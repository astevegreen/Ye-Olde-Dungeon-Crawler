## Goal
Wave 9 paints the bestiary's portraits in four batches. Each batch is drawn first in the art bible (`.prompts/character-art-bible/bible/js/m-portraits*.js`), and ported only after the owner approves its contact sheet (owner: "All recommendations approved"). 9a, 9b and 9c are done and pushed. 9d is "the other 18 families": one portrait per regular monster family, recoloured for its variants (`.prompts/claude-character-art-overhaul.md:131, :239`). It is done when the families are drawn, the sheet is approved, the portraits are ported and registered with fidelity identical, the gates are green, and the change is pushed. **Local only:** the bible, the sheets and the fidelity tool live in the gitignored `.prompts/` on the owner's machine, so a cloud session can't do this.

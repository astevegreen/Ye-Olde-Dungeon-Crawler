## Decisions
- "Bosses take half" is the chasm rule that already exists (`impulse.ts`: a boss takes half its max HP and stays); no new rule was added. Chosen because that's how the plan's sentence reads.
- Pull moves one tile at a time, re-aimed each step, with `applyImpulse(..., 1, 1)`. The new `maxDistance` cap means the pusher's knockback bonus can't splat a pulled foe into the caster.
- Mark is a built-in `marked` status (potency is the percent, 10 when unset). The engine also gains two generic hooks for status handlers: `onDamageTaken` and `sensedThroughWalls`. Melee and spell damage both go through `Entity.takeDamage` → `loseHp`.
- Aimed teleport is `teleport` with `aimed: true`. `EffectContext.aim` carries the tile aimed at. An occupied tile puts the caster down on the nearest free tile beside it.

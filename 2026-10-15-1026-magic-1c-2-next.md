## Decisions
- 1c split chosen: 1c-1 = resolver, rules, report and tests; 1c-2 = cast wiring, cache and dry run.
- Opposite bonus counts once per cast, however many opposite neighbours. Blood-price and opposite bonuses add (+50%), not multiply. Chosen as the simplest reading; tell the owner at the dry run.
- Doubling: a flavour from one of the borrower's own elements, or anything from a doubled slot (Týr, Loki), uses the `doubled` form; never doubled twice. Pack-set `lends` (Blood-price, frith) never double by element.
- A forged arcane element lends plain `arcane` (Magic Arrow's pierce); the base arcane element lends `arcane:<school>`.
- A spell with no `spellType` borrows nothing but still lends.
- `resolvedCasts` is exempt from the diagnostics flight-recorder wrap (`READ_ONLY` in `src/engine/debug/triage.ts`).

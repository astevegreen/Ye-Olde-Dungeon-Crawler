# Item Families & Pack Art

> Topic doc under [content-extensibility.md](content-extensibility.md) ([ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) §3). Explanatory only; every binding rule lives in `ARCHITECTURE.md` (Pack-Neutral Presentation among them). Flag a conflict rather than resolving it here (§8.2).

## Item Families
Equipment found as loot may belong to one of seven families — Blessed, Enchanted, Holy (positive), Cursed, Hexed, Unholy (negative), Chaotic — in addition to its separate +N roll ([ADR-0012](../decisions/0012-item-families-as-pack-data.md)). The engine owns the family ids, the three alignments and what each modifier field does; `manifest.itemFamilies` (`ItemFamilyConfig`) supplies the pack's tiers (names, numbers, the floor each starts on), each family's expected count per game, its first floor and whether it binds, and `itemsPerGame`, the measured number of eligible items a game offers. `rollItemFamily` (`src/engine/items/modifierRoller.ts`) rolls Normal first, then a family by `perGame / itemsPerGame`, then the deepest tier the floor has reached; a family below its `minFloor` gives its share to Normal. Tiers that share the deepest floor reached are variants, one picked at random (cotw's eight Chaotic effects all start on floor 1). Only loot rolls: `createScaledItem` applies the config when a caller passes it (floor loot, chests, vault chests, the boss hoard, monster drops), and shop stock, starting gear, choice rewards and kill-rite essences stay Normal. `ItemDefinition.family` pins a definition to one family, its tier by floor. A pack without `itemFamilies` gets only Normal items and an unchanged loot stream.

## What a Modifier Does
What a modifier does is its fields, read in one place each (`ItemModifier`): melee and spell scaling, mana discount, damage taken, `tagBonuses` against a defender tag (`living` is "not undead"; a bonus may heal the attacker a share, `healPercentOfDamage`), `binds`, `sacredGroundBurn` (the bearer burns stepping onto or striking from sacred ground — a `TileDefinition.sacred` altar or a `TownBuildingDefinition.sacred` temple, `isSacredGround`) and `templeShunned` (the temple serves only the cleanse, at double the price; `TownServicesDefinition.templeShunnedMessage`). Damage taken (`damageTakenMultiplier`) and healing received scale in `Actor.takeDamage`/`Actor.heal`, so they apply whatever dealt the blow or the cure.

The rule-benders the Chaotic family is made of: `randomSpellElement`, `killHealPercent`, `forbidsRest`, `extraMeleeStrikes`/`missSelfDamage`, `evasionBonus`, `blinkEverySteps`/`blinkRange`, `meleeDamageRoll`, `overflowNoDebt`/`overflowTierShift`/`overflowTierShiftCap`, `reflectMeleePercent`, `healingReceivedMultiplier`.

The perk effects added by tracker 3.6 (any item may carry them too): `belowHalfHpMeleeMultiplier`, `manaCostMultiplier`, `perceptionRadiusMultiplier`, `meleeDamageTakenMultiplier`, `impulseImmune`, `killManaPercent`, `overflowDebtDecayMultiplier`, `carryMultiplier` (`Player.carryStrength`), `knockbackBonus`, `onHitStatus`, `rangedHitBonus`, `rangedDamageBonus` (no cotw perk uses them: nothing fires a ranged attack in play), `meleeHitBonus`, `shortenedAfflictions` (`afflictionDuration`, read by melee bites and hook procs), `identifiesCarriedOnStairs` (`identifyCarriedSinceStairs`, on the stairs), `sightBonus` (`Player.sightBonus`, added to the FOV radius in `GameEngine.updateFov`; [ADR-0013](../decisions/0013-sight-bonus-in-fov-radius.md)), `followUpStrikeShare` (a half-damage second strike), `elementSpellMultiplier` (spells of one element), `resistsElements` (`Actor.affinityTo`: neutral becomes resisted, a weakness neutral; every element-affinity read goes through `affinityTo`), `grimoireSynergyRepeats` (each neighbor synergy counts again), `critChanceBonus`/`critMultiplier` (melee criticals in a pack with no base crit), `companionStatMultiplier` (`bondCompanion`, once per companion on its turn) and `companionRisesPerFloor`, `lastStandPerFloor` (both in `refusesDeath`, which `DeathResolver.resolveDeath` asks first; "once per floor" is once per visit, `floorVisitKey`), `sensesAllMonsters` (`sensesThroughWalls`, which the renderer asks beside Detect Monsters), `spellRangeBonus` (`withSpellRangeBonus`, at cast and in `resolveCast`), `defensePenetration` (beside Anatomist's half), `shieldWithTwoHanded` (`Paperdoll.shieldBesideTwoHanded`, installed by `Player`; `Paperdoll.displacedBy` is the one place a two-hander's blocking is decided), `ripostesOnEvade` and `evadeBlinkRange` (`MeleeAttackAction.afterEvade`), `evasionPerIntelligence` (`intelligenceEvasion`), `trapImmune` (`TrapInstance.trigger`, and an impulse onto a trap tile), `restHealMultiplier` (`AutoRestManager.recoverRestTurn`, both rest paths), `grantsStatusImmunities` (added to `statusImmunities` when a perk is granted; worn, it holds only while worn: `Actor.isImmuneTo`, which `StatusManager.applyStatus` asks beside the caller's list), `chainExtraHops`, `firstSpellPerFloorMultiplier` (`wardFirstSpell`), `maxHpPercent` (`Player.wornMaxHpPercent` in `calculateAttribute`; a choice granting it raises HP with the max), `levelUpFullHeal`, and a negative `overflowTierShift` (a milder surge; below the first tier none).

Those a family perk scopes to one monster family (`familyModifiers`): `meleeKnockback`, `sensesWithin`, `coinMultiplier`, `xpMultiplier`, `afflictionShrugChance` (`shrugsAffliction`, asked on every path a monster afflicts by: a melee bite, a spell's status, a hook), `windUpWarningBonus`, `windUpDamageTakenMultiplier` (a breath or slam), `wakeRadius` (also worn or held: `wakesOnSight`, [ADR-0014](../decisions/0014-wake-radius-in-fov-awakening.md)), besides `damageTakenMultiplier` and `defensePenetration` from the family.

## Worn Effects
A definition can give its item any of these fields outright, family or not: `ItemDefinition.wornEffects` (a `PerkEffects`), which `wornModifiers` adds while the item is worn. cotw's Ring of the Slag-Walker, Zealot's Seared Crown and the Obsidian Scale Cuirass resist fire, the Soot-Visored Helm keeps blindness off, the Rootless Striders paralysis and traps, the Corpse-Chieftain's Eye-Coin stuns; the Pit-Draugr Pick and the Quicksilver Stiletto pierce armour (`defensePenetration`), the Ironwood Bough-Stave raises spell damage, the Amber Heart-Drop max HP and Thrym's girdle the carrying Strength (owner, 2026-10-07). `itemDescriptions.test.ts` fails a description that promises a resistance or immunity without one.

## Sprites
A `spriteRecipes` key equal to a definition ID gives that entity its own atlas cell (`SpriteAtlas`). The renderer's built-in cells and tag rules (`DEFAULT_TAG_SPRITE_ORDER`) cover only generic archetypes; a pack's own creature tags map to its recipes through `atlas.spriteTagRules`, checked first, and its zone and town-exterior terrain (`<base>_<zoneKey>`, `floor_town_outdoor`) are its own recipes too.

## Terrain
A pack that declares `atlas.terrain` gets neighbour-aware terrain from `src/rendering/atlas/terrain-layers.ts`: floors pick among tone variants by smooth world-space noise (optionally a 2x2 macro tile), walls take a face when open to the south and rim or corner overlays on other open sides, liquids and pits blend as one area with edges toward other tiles, and pillars, bars, stairs, traps, doors and the pack's own tiles draw as props over the floor. Doors between rock east and west sit in the wall line; doors never cast a contact shadow, so a corridor joins its room without a break. Terrain cells bake flat (no sprite outline or diagonal shading), `terrain.memory` replaces the blue-slate dimming of remembered cells, and `terrain.torch`/`emissive` light visible cells (the player's Torchlight setting turns it off; a lit town, `town.lit`, keeps only the emissive glow).

Without `atlas.terrain`, a pack keeps one recipe per tile type (`getTerrainSpriteKey`), which paints water, chasms, bars and pillars as floor; `drawTacticalTerrain` marks those over it in the theme's role colors (it no longer picks looks by floor number), as it does any such cell the pack's terrain recipes miss.

## Surface and Gas Overlays
Surfaces and gases are the pack's art too: `atlas.overlays` maps `surface~<type>` and `gas~<type>` to a `CellOverlayArt`, which, unlike a recipe, is not baked into the atlas but runs every frame over the terrain, in canvas units at the cell's place, given the cell and the frame's clock so fire can flicker without content reading the clock (cotw's are in `terrain/overlays.ts`). `IntentOverlay.renderSurfaces` draws a type with no art as a neutral wash (the theme's `warn` for a surface, `textSoft` for a gas), so a surface or gas the pack draws no art for never acts unseen.
